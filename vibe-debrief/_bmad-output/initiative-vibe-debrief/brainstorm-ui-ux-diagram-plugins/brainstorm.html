Three Core Breakthroughs

Where all 16 techniques converged at the close of the session.

Breakthrough 01

Spatial Separation & Plugin Orchestra

Stop forcing Vibe Debrief's 360px iframe to be both text summarizer and full diagram editor; keep panel.html as the crisp Cockpit HUD + fast native multi-grammar SVG preview, and delegate heavy editable boards (Excalidraw), interactive simulations (Visualize Gen UI), and decks (Presentations / Deckcraft) via a unified Diagram Spec + Plugin Visual Dock.

Plain Rule: Side panel stays fast & light; heavy canvases open in the big stage on click.
Breakthrough 02

Zero-Scroll Progressive Disclosure

Replace the 4-row header and endlessly scrolling 4-card wall with a 1-strip compact header, a 3-Second / 30-Second / 3-Minute Zoom Ladder, a horizontal 5-step pill stepper (1.Why 2.Plan 3.Proof 4.Risks 5.Learn) with live verification/risk badge counters, and X-Ray toggle (Plain English <-> Exact Code).

Plain Rule: Grasp truth in 3s, inspect proof in 30s, dive deep only when you ask.
Breakthrough 03

Adaptive Response Lenses + Anytime Micro-Animation

Auto-detect response shape (Debug, Build, Concept, Comparison, Plan) and diagram grammar (Sequence, FlowDiff, Subway Layer Map, Before/After Split, Decision Tree) while unlocking inline kinetic SVG flow + on-demand Canvas Curator Tour for every diagram.

Plain Rule: Diagrams morph to fit the answer's shape, and any diagram can animate on click.
